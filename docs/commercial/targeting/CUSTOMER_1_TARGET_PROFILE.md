# Customer #1 Target Profile

Target enterprise-oriented B2B AI SaaS/Agent providers, not generic AI users. High-fit products have
public evidence of tool/API/MCP/connector authority, customer or enterprise-data access, RAG,
delegated credentials, or external/write actions, alongside enterprise sales/security-review pressure.

Preferred roles: Founder/CEO (smaller firms), CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Security/CISO, Head of
Product, or enterprise/security executive. Use only public professional routes; do not collect private
personal data. Initial batch is 20–30 companies; only the top five receive deep manual review.
