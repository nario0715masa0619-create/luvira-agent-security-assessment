# Explainable Customer #1 Lead Scoring

Score only with public evidence. Record FACT, INFERENCE, or UNKNOWN; inference never becomes fact and
UNKNOWN earns no points. Hard exclusion overrides numeric score.

| Dimension | Points | Public evidence examples |
|---|---:|---|
| Technical ASA Fit | 30 | B2B agent/product, tools, connectors, RAG/API documentation |
| Enterprise/Security Review Pressure | 25 | enterprise plan, trust/security page, SSO/SCIM/RBAC, questionnaire language |
| Authority/Data Exposure | 20 | data access, token/connector scope, action capability, multi-tenant deployment |
| Commercial Ability to Pay | 15 | enterprise motion, established product/customer signals; no unsupported estimate |
| Timing/Trigger Event | 10 | agent launch, integrations, enterprise expansion, security hiring/positioning |

Classes: A Priority 80–100; B Strong 65–79; C Watch 50–64; D Low Fit below 50; EXCLUDE overrides.
Exclude no meaningful agent product, consumer-only/simple chatbot, no relevant authority/data surface,
consultancy rather than applicable product, out-of-scope product, or insufficient public evidence.
