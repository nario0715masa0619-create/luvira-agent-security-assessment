# First Customer Launch Checklist

- [ ] ICP, offer, price approval, scope/exclusions, proposal, sample output, claims policy, and qualification ready
- [ ] SOW inputs, legal-review status, payment process, and GTM-2 handoff known

Outcome: **READY_TO_SELL**, **READY_TO_SELL_WITH_CONDITIONS**, or **NOT_READY_TO_SELL**.

Commercial recommendation for human approval: fixed-price Design Partner pilot at **JPY 500,000
excluding tax**, normally ~2 weeks after start conditions; future standard hypothesis **JPY
1,000,000–2,000,000+**, consistent with existing canonical pricing. Re-quote for multiple products/
environments, large tool/connector inventory, complex roles/tenancy, production-only work, unusual
sensitivity, extra test cases/retest, or short deadline. Retest recommendation: separately scoped and
quoted. Payment timing, tax, cancellation, proposal validity, final price, and legal terms remain
commercial/legal decisions.
