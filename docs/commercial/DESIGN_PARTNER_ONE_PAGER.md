# Luvira Agent Security Assessment — Design Partner Program

**For:** B2B AI SaaS/Agent providers facing enterprise security review, questionnaire pressure, or
the need to explain what an AI system can actually do through tools, permissions, connectors,
credentials, and data boundaries.

ASA is an evidence-backed, action-centric assessment: it evaluates not only what the AI says, but
what it can access, what tools it can use, what external actions it can trigger, and under whose
authority — connecting Agent identity, user/customer permissions, credentials, tools, connectors/
SaaS/APIs, and target resources to business impact. It produces bounded Findings, business-impact
explanation, root cause/trust-boundary analysis, remediation guidance, a customer report, and a
walkthrough. It does not guarantee security, certification, complete coverage, remediation
implementation, managed remediation, or continuous monitoring.

ASA is differentiated by architecture understanding, authority/permission reasoning, evidence-backed
Findings, business-impact translation, remediation-ready output, and enterprise sales enablement —
not by the number of attack patterns, checklist size, or prompt injection library breadth.

ASA may use AI to help organize information, draft risk hypotheses, and draft report language. AI
output is candidate/draft only: final Findings, Severity, Confidence, and customer-facing conclusions
always require evidence review, human review, and QA (see
[AI-assisted Assessment Controls](../operations/AI_ASSISTED_ASSESSMENT_CONTROLS.md)).

**Design Partner hypothesis:** one AI product, normally one test/staging environment, a selected ASA
v1 Test Case subset, and approximately two weeks after all start conditions are met. The synthetic
[sample Finding](../examples/SYNTHETIC_SAMPLE_FINDING.md) is illustrative only, not a customer result.
