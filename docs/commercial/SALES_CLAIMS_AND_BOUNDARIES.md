# Sales Claims and Boundaries

Allowed: evidence-backed assessment; authority/tool/connector risk assessment; remediation-ready
Findings; support for enterprise security conversations and questionnaire evidence preparation.

Forbidden: complete security, all vulnerabilities, certification/SOC2/ISO outcome, no breach, 100%
safety, unsupported market leadership, fake customers/results. ASA v1 diagnoses and provides guidance;
implementation is a future separate service and managed remediation is not included.
