# Authorization to Test — First-Customer Template

This operational template is not legal advice or a substitute for a reviewed SOW/contract.
Qualified legal review is required before customer use.

## Required written approval

Record customer legal entity, authorized representative, Luvira legal entity, approval date, and
sign-off. The representative confirms authority over every listed target; vague consent such as
“test everything” is not authorization.

## Authorized scope

List exact product/system, domains/endpoints, accounts, staging/production environment, start/end
window, approved ASA attack families, rate/volume limits, permitted tool/connector actions and
data-access boundaries. State whether production testing is separately approved.

## Prohibitions and boundaries

List prohibited actions, irreversible/write restrictions, credential-use boundaries, third-party
SaaS/API boundaries, external communication restrictions, and sensitive-data constraints.
Customer authorization does not itself authorize intrusive testing of a third-party system.

## Safety and escalation

Name emergency-stop authority and contacts. Acknowledge testing risk, stop conditions, customer
rollback ownership, and that restart after STOP requires renewed required approval.
