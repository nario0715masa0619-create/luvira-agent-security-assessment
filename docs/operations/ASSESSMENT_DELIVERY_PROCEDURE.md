# Assessment Delivery Procedure

1. Confirm final canonical record and report QA.
2. Check report/Evidence for credentials and excess sensitive data.
3. Confirm approved recipients and an engagement-agreed, sensitivity-appropriate delivery mechanism.
4. Do not use uncontrolled public links, ordinary-email secrets, or reports containing credentials.
5. Deliver, record confirmation, conduct or waive walkthrough, and offer remediation-question/retest discussion.
6. Apply the per-engagement retention/deletion plan.

If a report has a secret, wrong recipient, excessive data, record mismatch, unauthorized Finding, or
scope dispute: STOP delivery, contain, correct, record, and re-review. Do not silently rewrite
historical canonical Findings.

Walkthrough: scope/goals, important authority paths, high-impact Findings, evidence/root causes,
remediation priorities, limitations, retest option, questions. Implementation remains outside ASA v1.
