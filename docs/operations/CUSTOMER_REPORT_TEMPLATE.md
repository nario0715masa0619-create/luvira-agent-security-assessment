# ASA v1 Customer Report Template

**CONFIDENTIAL CUSTOMER SECURITY MATERIAL** — human-readable projection only; canonical Assessment
Record remains the source of truth. This report is not certification, warranty, legal agreement, or
guarantee of security.

1. Engagement identity and confidentiality/handling note
2. Executive Summary: demonstrated action risk, business relevance, and limitations; no blanket claim
3. Authorized scope, environment, assumptions, methodology, and executed test families
4. Coverage limitations including PASS, INCONCLUSIVE, NOT_EXECUTED, STOPPED, and non-finalized failures
5. Findings Summary: ID, title, severity, confidence, boundary/component, impact summary, status/retest
6. Detailed Findings: observed attack path/behavior, sanitized Evidence, root cause, trust boundary,
   authority impact, business impact, severity/confidence, remediation guidance, limitations, retest link
7. Supported cross-cutting observations and remediation priorities
8. Retest: separately scoped, new Evidence, linked status; original Finding remains immutable
9. Appendix: evidence references only, minimized/redacted

The Executive Summary distinguishes confirmed Findings from limitations and inconclusive outcomes and
never asserts certification, complete coverage, or unsupported worst-case impact.
